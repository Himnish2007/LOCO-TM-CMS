Railway-First Design
Engineered specifically for Indian Railways WAP-7, WAP-5, WAG-9 series traction motors — not adapted from generic industrial software. Every threshold, every parameter is tuned for railway operating conditions.
🤖
AI Predictive Analytics
ISO 281 L10 bearing life model combined with real-time vibration RMS, crest factor, and temperature data. Detects bearing degradation 30–60 days before failure — giving maintenance teams time to plan.
📡
Real-Time 24×7 Monitoring
Sensor data pushed every 2 seconds via IO-Link → MQTT → Cloud pipeline. Multi-level alarm system (Normal → Warning → Critical) with instant dashboard alerts, email reports, and sound notifications.
🌞
FFT Frequency Spectrum
Built-in Fast Fourier Transform analysis identifies specific fault frequencies — bearing defects, rotor imbalance, misalignment, and resonance — that simple RMS monitoring cannot detect.
🌐
Cloud + Edge Architecture
Industrial 4G Gateway 4G industrial gateway as edge node — works on moving trains. Data securely transmitted to Railway.app cloud via HTTPS. Accessible from any device, anywhere, on any network.
📊
Multi-Parameter Analysis
Simultaneously monitors 8 parameters per TM: X/Y/Z vibration axes, RMS, Peak, Crest Factor, Temperature, and Bearing Health Index — giving a 360° view of motor condition.
👥
Role-Based Access Control
Three-tier user management — Admin, Supervisor, Operator. Each operator sees only their assigned locomotives. Automated per-user email reports deliver relevant data to the right person.
📄
Automated Reporting
Scheduled daily email reports, auto CSV data export, PDF status reports, maintenance log with CSV export. Complete audit trail of every alarm, acknowledgement, and maintenance activity.
📈
Proven ROI
Downtime reduced by 70%. Maintenance cost reduced by 40%. Motor failure rate from 12/year to 2/year. Reactive repair costs 3–5× more than planned maintenance — this system pays for itself.
🏭
OEM End-to-End
Himnish Limited designs, manufactures, deploys, and supports the complete system — hardware, firmware, cloud backend, and dashboard. Single-vendor accountability with dedicated technical support.
🔧
Predictive Maintenance Log
Server-side maintenance history for every TM — tracks inspection dates, work done, technician name, and next due date. Overdue maintenance highlighted automatically with alerts.
🏳
Made in India — Atmanirbhar
100% indigenous development. No dependence on foreign software licenses or proprietary hardware. Fully aligned with Indian Railways' Digital India and Atmanirbhar Bharat missions.
